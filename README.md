The Midnight Vault — a one-night heist at the Aurelia Gallery. Cross a guarded room past sweeping vision cones and security cameras, crack the glass case holding the Aurora Diamond with a lockpick, grab the side gems on the way, and slip out the service door before the alarm hits 100%. Smoke pellets, EMP bursts, and tossed coins are your tools, and every one of them costs a charge.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move, Shift to sneak, E (hold) to crack the case, 1 for smoke, 2 for EMP, click to toss a coin and distract a guard.
